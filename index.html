Who We Are

The Rotaract E-Club of Harmony

By leveraging digital platforms, we empower our members to connect, serve, and make a positive impact from anywhere in the world — truly connecting hearts and creating impacts.

The Four-Way Test

…of the things we think, say or do:

1

Is it the truth?

2

Is it fair to all concerned?

3

Will it build goodwill and better friendships?

4

Will it be beneficial to all concerned?

Purpose of Rotaract

The purpose of Rotaract is to provide an opportunity for young men and women to enhance the knowledge and skills that will assist them in personal development, to address the physical and social needs of their communities, and to promote better relations between all people worldwide through a framework of friendship and service.

Goals of Rotaract

  • To develop professional and leadership skills.
  • To emphasize respect for the rights of others, and to promote ethical standards and the dignity of all useful occupations.
  • To provide opportunities for young people to address the needs and concerns of the community and our world.
  • To provide opportunities for working in cooperation with sponsoring Rotary clubs.
  • To motivate young people for eventual membership in Rotary.
Our Story

A Journey of Harmony

December 6, 2023

The Vision Takes Shape

The Rotaract E-Club of Harmony was formed by passionate Rotaractors and friends who shared a vision of promoting fellowship, leadership, and meaningful community service through a flexible online platform. Many pioneer members came from the Rotaract Clubs of the University of Ilorin and LAUTECH, bringing valuable experience and a passion for service.

March 4, 2024

Officially Chartered

After months of planning and collaboration, the club was officially chartered with 15 charter members. Since then, our membership has grown to 25 active members, reflecting our commitment to a welcoming and engaging environment for young leaders.

Present Day

Serving Without Borders

As an e-club, we give members the flexibility to connect, serve, and grow from anywhere. Through WhatsApp, Zoom, Facebook, X, Instagram, and more, our members remain actively involved in fellowship, leadership development, and impactful service projects — leaving every community better than we found it.
